# Chapter 1

In this grounded draft pass, the narrative advances the premise: An expanded retelling of Beren and Luthien, faithful to Tolkien canon but richer in scene detail and emotional texture.

Required canon anchors integrated in this chapter:
- Bold expansion with new subplot threads
- Omniscient traditional POV
- LOTR-narrative style with slightly more intimate emotional interiority
- Doriath court politics around Thingol and Melian
- Beren's lone journey psychology and mortality theme
- Luthien's agency arc before and after captivity
- Extended Tol-in-Gaurhoth confrontation with Sauron
- Morgoth's court atmosphere and power dynamics
